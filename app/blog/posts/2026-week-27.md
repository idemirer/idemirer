## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 4 July](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-4-july)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 4 July.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Washington, D.C. saw the highest increases in ADR (+34.8% to US$209.40) and RevPAR (+57.3% to US$149.78) with a lift from the America 250 celebrations.
- Detroit reported the largest occupancy gain (+23.7% to 63.4%) and the second-highest jump in RevPAR (+41.0% to US$76.87).
- Philadelphia, which hosted America 250 events and a World Cup match, registered the second-largest increase in ADR (+29.7% to US$188.91) and the third-highest lift in RevPAR (+38.9% to US$123.35).
- Overall, 21 of the Top 25 Markets registered an increase in RevPAR.

28 June - 4 July 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 63.5% (+3.9%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$167.95 (+6.7%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$106.66 (+10.9%)
