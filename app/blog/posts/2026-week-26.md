## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 27 June](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-27-june)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 27 June.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Miami saw the highest increases in ADR (+51.1% to US$267.87) and RevPAR (+51.6% to US$196.87) with a lift from three World Cup matches. The market’s occupancy stayed relatively flat (+0.3% to 73.5%).
- San Diego, which hosted the 2026 BIO International Convention, reported the largest jump in occupancy (+13.1% to 91.9%).
- San Francisco, host of two World Cup matches, registered the second-largest gains in occupancy (+10.9% to 82.5%) and RevPAR (+40.7% to US$211.19).
- Overall, 20 of the Top 25 Markets registered an increase in RevPAR.

21-27 June 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 72.2% (+1.2%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$178.82 (+9.2%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$129.09 (+9.6%)
