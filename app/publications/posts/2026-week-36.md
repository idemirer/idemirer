## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 5 September](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-5-september)

The U.S. hotel industry reported a 21st consecutive week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 5 September.

- Year-over-year growth was elevated by the Labor Day calendar shift.
- Among the Top 25 Markets, Minneapolis saw the largest gains in occupancy (+23.2% to 64.9%) and RevPAR (+35.7% to US$87.52).
- The highest ADR increase was recorded in Las Vegas (+24.8% to US$202.85).
- St. Louis reported the steepest declines across each of the three key performance metrics: occupancy (-7.4% to 57.7%), ADR (-4.7% to US$123.10) and RevPAR (-11.8% to US$71.01).

30 Aug - 5 Sept 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 63.0% (+9.4%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$159.19 (+6.1%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$100.31 (+16.1%)
