## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 26 September](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-26-september)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 26 September.

- Performance growth was elevated on the positive side of the Rosh Hashanah calendar shift.
- Among the Top 25 Markets, Orlando saw the largest occupancy increase (+18.6% to 68.3%).
- Miami reported the highest rise in ADR (+19.8% to US$177.65).
- San Francisco registered the largest RevPAR lift (+35.1% to US$223.16).
- Phoenix was the only Top 25 Market with a decrease in RevPAR (-0.5% to US$99.09).

20-26 Sept 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 69.7% (+6.4%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$179.43 (+7.7%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$125.06 (14.2%)
