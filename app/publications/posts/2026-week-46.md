## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 14 November](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-14-november)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 14 November.

- U.S. airport hotels saw the largest performance gains among location types due to the flight disruptions that started on Friday, 13 November. RevPAR in that segment grew 38.4% and 29.2% on Friday and Saturday, respectively.
- Among the Top 25 Markets, New Orleans reported the highest occupancy increase (+13.9% to 67.6%). The market’s performance was helped by the We Belong Here Festival.
- Miami registered the largest gains in ADR (+22.3% to US$256.11) and RevPAR (+32.2% to US$215.46). The market hosted E1 Miami GP and the Miami Beach Polo World Cup.
- Oahu Island was the only market to report a RevPAR decline (-14.3% to US$185.00).

8-14 Nov 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 64.5% (+6.0%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$164.85 (+6.5%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$106.37 (+12.9%)
