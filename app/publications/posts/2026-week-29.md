## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 18 July](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-18-july)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 18 July.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, New York City saw the largest increases in ADR (+41.5% to US$425.03) and RevPAR (+40.3% to US$377.07). The market reported a 105.1% lift in ADR and a 116.1% gain in RevPAR on Saturday, 18 July – the night before the World Cup final between Spain and Argentina.
- Washington, D.C., reported the largest occupancy gain (+7.0% to 78.3%).
- Helped by continued World Cup tourism, Miami registered the second-highest jump in ADR (+26.7% to US$212.74).
- Dallas, which hosted a semifinal match between France and Spain, experienced the second-largest RevPAR increase (+26.6% to US$96.81) on the third-highest ADR gain (+21.5% to US$144.41).

12-18 July 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 72.4% (+1.1%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$174.49 (+5.2%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$126.33 (+6.3%)
