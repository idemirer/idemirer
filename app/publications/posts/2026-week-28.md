## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 11 July](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-11-july)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 11 July.

- Among CoStar’s top 25 markets, Miami saw the highest increases in ADR (+37.6 percent to $232.47) and RevPAR (+38.0 percent to $157.00) thanks to the World Cup quarterfinal match between England and Norway on July 11.
- Detroit reported the largest occupancy gain (+9.3 percent to 70.2 percent).
- Helped by multiple events, including the WFG Convention and the World Series of Poker Main Event, Las Vegas registered the second-highest jump in RevPAR (+23.9 percent to $149.80).
- Boston, host of the World Cup quarterfinal between France and Morocco on July 9, posted the second-largest ADR increase (+21.8 percent to $279.21).

5-11 July 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 67.6% (+0.7%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$166.04 (+4.5%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$112.18 (+5.2%)
