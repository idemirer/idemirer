## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 3 October](https://www.hotelnewsresource.com/article143246.html)

The U.S. hotel industry recorded year-over-year growth during the week of Sept. 27 through Oct. 3, supported in part by the favorable timing of Yom Kippur, according to CoStar data.

- Among the Top 25 hotel markets, Orlando posted the largest occupancy increase, rising 17.3% to 66%.
- Miami led the major markets in rate and revenue growth. Its ADR increased 23.5% to $186.73, while RevPAR rose 43.3% to $123.25.
- Las Vegas was the only Top 25 market to report declines across all three measures. Occupancy fell 5.5% to 77.2%, ADR dropped 12.8% to $204.40, and RevPAR declined 17.6% to $157.78.
- The Las Vegas comparison was tied to the timing of PACK EXPO, which took place during the comparable period in 2025.

27 Sept - 3 Oct 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 67.2% (+5.5%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$172.38 (+7.0%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$115.84 (+12.9%)
