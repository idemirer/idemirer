## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 25 July](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-25-july)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 25 July.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, New York City saw the largest increases in ADR (+28.1% to US$375.12) and RevPAR (+35.0% to US$348.62). The market reported a 142.2% lift in ADR and a 179.6% gain in RevPAR on Sunday, 19 July – the night of the World Cup final between Spain and Argentina.
- Philadelphia reported the largest occupancy gain (+9.0% to 77.1%).
- Las Vegas experienced the steepest declines across each of the three key performance metrics: occupancy (-15.9% to 65.7%), ADR (-5.4% to US$176.38) and RevPAR (-20.4% to US$115.88). The drop in performance was mainly due to a comparison against Beyoncé and Backstreet Boys concerts held last year.

19-25 July 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 72.5% (+1.3%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$173.47 (+4.9%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$125.72 (+6.3%)
