## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 7 November](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-7-november)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 7 November.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, St. Louis reported the highest increases in occupancy (+7.5% to 68.5%) and RevPAR (+18.6% to US$97.73). The market hosted the 18th Church of God in Christ International Holy Convocation.
- Miami reported the largest ADR lift (+11.6% to US$245.59).
- Overall, 15 of the Top 25 Markets posted a RevPAR increase. Total U.S. performance was mainly driven by markets outside of the Top 25 with a RevPAR gain of 7.1%.

1-7 Nov 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 65.1% (+1.4%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$168.35 (+3.2%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$109.55 (+4.7%)
