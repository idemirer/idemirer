## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 15 August](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-15-august)

The U.S. hotel industry reported an 18th consecutive week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 15 August.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, San Diego saw the highest increases in ADR (+12.6% to US$249.71) and RevPAR (+22.8% to US$201.10). The market’s performance was helped by LPL Focus 2026 as well as a Jason Aldean and Luke Bryan concert.
- Tampa reported the only double-digit occupancy lift (+11.7% to 64.9%) and the second-highest RevPAR gain (+17.5% to US$96.55). The market hosted the DoDIIS Worldwide Conference and a Mötley Crüe concert.

9-15 August 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 68.0% (+2.6%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$163.56 (+3.5%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$111.29 (+6.2%)
