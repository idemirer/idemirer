## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 24 October](https://www.hotelnewsresource.com/article143538.html)

The U.S. hotel industry recorded year-over-year gains during the week of Oct. 18-24, with occupancy, average daily rate and revenue per available room increasing.

- Among the Top 25 U.S. hotel markets, Tampa posted the largest occupancy gain, rising 14.8% year over year to 72.6%.
- Chicago recorded the highest gains in average daily rate and RevPAR. ADR rose 26.4% to US$235.51, while RevPAR rose 39.7% to US$189.26, aided by PACK EXPO International.
- Oahu recorded the steepest declines in occupancy and RevPAR. Occupancy fell 10.2% to 72.2%, while RevPAR decreased 10.7% to US$185.74.
- Anaheim posted the largest decline in average daily rate, down 8.0% to US$197.55.

18-24 Oct 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 69.8% (+4.8%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$179.55 (+7.4%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$125.31 (+12.6%)
