## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 10 October](https://eglobaltravelmedia.com.au/2026/10/18/u-s-hotels-gain-momentum-as-revpar-jumps-6-5/)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 10 October.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, San Francisco saw the largest increases across each of the three key performance metrics: occupancy (+10.6% to 88.5%), ADR (+18.5% to US$274.02) and RevPAR (+31.1% to US$242.53). The market was host to both Tech Week and Fleet Week.
- Chicago registered the second-highest gains in occupancy (+6.8% to 82.7%) and RevPAR (+21.1% to US$210.18). The market’s performance was mostly due to the APTA TRANSform & EXPO and the ACEP Scientific Assembly.
- Overall, 17 of the Top 25 Markets reported a RevPAR increase.

4-10 Oct 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 70.6% (+2.3%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$179.58 (+4.1%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$126.79 (+6.5%)
