## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 1 August](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-1-august)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 1 August.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Philadelphia saw the highest increases in ADR (+19.6% to US$175.02) and RevPAR (+32.1% to US$137.55). The market’s performance was helped by two Morgan Wallen concerts.
- St. Louis reported the largest occupancy lift (+15.1% to 75.4%) and the second-highest gains in ADR (+13.5% to US$140.98) and RevPAR (+30.6% to US$106.27).
- Las Vegas experienced the steepest declines across each of the three key performance metrics: occupancy (-11.7% to 66.2%), ADR (-6.2% to US$145.87) and RevPAR (-17.1% to US$96.63).

26 July - 1 August 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 71.3% (+2.6%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$168.82 (+4.5%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$120.45 (+7.3%)
