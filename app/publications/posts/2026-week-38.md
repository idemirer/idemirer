## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 19 September](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-19-september)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 19 September.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Chicago saw the largest gains in ADR (+28.2% to US$257.66) and RevPAR (+41.8% to US$226.18). The market’s performance was lifted by the International Manufacturing Technology Show.
- Washington, D.C. reported the highest rise in occupancy (+10.7% to 82.3%) and the second-largest RevPAR increase (+23.9% to US$194.23).
- Overall, 21 of the Top 25 Markets recorded a RevPAR lift.

13-19 Sept 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 71.1% (+4.4%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$179.30 (+5.7%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$127.43 (10.4%)
