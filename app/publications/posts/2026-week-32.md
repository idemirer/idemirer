## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 8 August](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-8-august)

The U.S. hotel industry reported another week of positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 8 August.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Philadelphia saw the highest increases in occupancy (+14.2% to 74.6%) and RevPAR (+27.4% to US$116.29) as well as the second-highest gain in ADR (+11.5% to US$155.79)
- Chicago reported the largest ADR lift (+15.2% to US$206.21) and the second-highest jump in RevPAR (+23.8% to US$176.93).
- Miami experienced the steepest declines in occupancy (-7.2% to 65.8%) and RevPAR (-8.5% to US$107.60), while the largest ADR decrease (-4.1% to US$158.12) was reported in Nashville.

2-8 August 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 70.0% (+3.0%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$166.85 (+4.1%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$116.77 (+7.2%)
