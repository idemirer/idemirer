## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 24 October](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-31-october)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 31 October.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Tampa saw the highest rise in occupancy (+15.9% to 69.5%) and the second-largest RevPAR increase (+25.4% to US$114.31). The market’s performance was helped by the World Series.
- New Orleans reported the highest gains in ADR (+16.2% to US$196.08) and RevPAR (+32.2% to US$137.86) with performance helped by several conferences at the beginning of the week.
- Overall, 20 of the Top 25 Markets posted a RevPAR increase.

25-31 Oct 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 61.8% (+4.3%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$164.95 (+5.2%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$101.88 (+9.7%)
