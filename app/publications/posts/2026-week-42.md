## [U.S. hotel results for week ending 17 October](https://www.costar.com/products/str-benchmark/resources/press-releases/us-hotel-results-week-ending-17-october)

The U.S. hotel industry reported positive year-over-year comparisons, according to CoStar’s latest data through 17 October.

- Among the Top 25 Markets, Detroit saw the largest increases in occupancy (+12.2% to 74.1%) and RevPAR (+20.0% to US$108.85). The market’s performance was helped by the Battery Show North America.
- San Diego posted the only double-digit ADR jump (+12.6% to US$246.06) and the second-highest RevPAR gain (+18.5% to US$199.14).
- San Francisco reported the steepest declines in ADR (-23.4% to US$283.18) and RevPAR (-21.2% to US$242.73) due to a comparison against Dreamforce dates in 2025.

11-17 Oct 2026 (percentage change from comparable week in 2025):

- Occupancy: 70.7% (+3.1%)
- Average daily rate (ADR): US$177.65 (+2.3%)
- Revenue per available room (RevPAR): US$125.53 (+5.5%)
